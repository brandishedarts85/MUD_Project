HUMAN
Origin: Sunspire Dominion

Humans are adaptable, ambitious, and capable of excelling in any role.
They begin with balanced minimums and receive a +1 bonus to any stat
of their choice during creation.

Starting Zone: Sunspire

Stat Identity:
  Minimums: All stats ≥ 8
  Bonus: +1 to any stat
  Strengths: Flexibility, customization
  Weaknesses: No innate specialization

Flavor:
“Ordinary in form, extraordinary in potential.”
