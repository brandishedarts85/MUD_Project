CLERIC

Role: Healer, protector, spiritual caster
Bonuses: +2 SPI, +1 PRE
Starting Items: Holy symbol or spiritual focus

Clerics channel spiritual energy to heal allies, smite foes, and
support their group. Their resilience and presence make them reliable
in any battle.

Recommended Builds:
  heavy   – +VIT synergy
  average – balanced support

Flavor:
“Light in their hands, faith in their stride.”
