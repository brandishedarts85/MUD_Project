WARRIOR

Role: Frontline melee combatant
Bonuses: +2 MIG, +1 VIT
Starting Items: Basic weapon and shield

Warriors rely on strength and durability to dominate close-quarters
combat. They excel at tanking, controlling enemies, and delivering
heavy physical damage.

Recommended Builds:
  muscular – best damage
  heavy    – best tanking

Flavor:
“Steel in hand, courage in heart.”
