DWARF
Origin: Obsidian Order Strongholds

Dwarves are sturdy, disciplined, and forged by stone and hardship.
They possess powerful vitality and reliable physical strength.

Starting Zone: Obsidian Order

Stat Identity:
  Minimums: MIG ≥ 10, VIT ≥ 10
  Bonuses: +2 VIT, +1 MIG
  Strengths: Durability, melee combat
  Weaknesses: Lower finesse

Flavor:
“Stone in their bones, iron in their resolve.”
