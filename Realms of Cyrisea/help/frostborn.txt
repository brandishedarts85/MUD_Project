FROSTBORN
Origin: Frostpeak Highlands

The Frostborn are descendants of ancient northern bloodlines, hardened
by blizzards and spiritual trials. Their vitality is unmatched.

Starting Zone: Frostpeak

Stat Identity:
  Minimums: VIT ≥ 12, SPI ≥ 10
  Bonuses: +2 VIT, +1 SPI
  Strengths: Survivability, spiritual power
  Weaknesses: Lower finesse and arcane aptitude

Flavor:
“Cold-forged bodies, warm and unbreakable spirits.”
