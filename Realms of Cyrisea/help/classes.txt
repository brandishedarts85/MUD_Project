CLASSES OF CYRISEA

Cyrisea offers four core classes, each with unique stat bonuses,
starting items, and combat roles.

  WARRIOR   – Frontline melee fighter; strength and vitality.
  RANGER    – Agile skirmisher; finesse and perception.
  MAGE      – Arcane spellcaster; high arcana.
  CLERIC    – Spiritual healer/support; spirit and presence.

Type HELP WARRIOR, HELP RANGER, HELP MAGE, or HELP CLERIC for details.
