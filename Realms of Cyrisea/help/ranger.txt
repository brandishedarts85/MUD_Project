RANGER

Role: Agile skirmisher and scout
Bonuses: +2 FIN, +1 PRE
Starting Items: Basic bow or light weapon

Rangers use finesse, perception, and mobility to strike precisely and
avoid danger. They excel at ranged combat, tracking, and ambushes.

Recommended Builds:
  frail    – hybrid ARC synergy
  average  – balanced performance

Flavor:
“Silent steps, sharp eyes, swift arrows.”
