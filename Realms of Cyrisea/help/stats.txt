STATS

MIG (Might)
  Physical strength, melee damage, carrying capacity.

FIN (Finesse)
  Accuracy, agility, ranged damage, precision.

VIT (Vitality)
  Health, endurance, physical resilience.

ARC (Arcana)
  Spell power, magical potency, arcane knowledge.

SPI (Spirit)
  Healing strength, spiritual magic, resistance to fear.

PRE (Presence)
  Awareness, perception, charisma, initiative.

Stats are modified by race, class, and body build.
