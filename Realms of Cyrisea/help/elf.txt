ELF
Origin: Crystalwood Expanse

Elves are swift, perceptive, and naturally attuned to arcane energies.
Their grace and focus make them exceptional rangers and mages.

Starting Zone: Crystalwood

Stat Identity:
  Minimums: FIN ≥ 10, ARC ≥ 10
  Bonuses: +2 FIN, +1 ARC
  Strengths: Accuracy, finesse, spellcasting
  Weaknesses: Lower vitality

Flavor:
“Born beneath shimmering leaves, their minds dance with quiet magic.”
